Major Replay Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. What we collect: only the sign-in permission (access token) TikTok gives the channel owner's own account, plus basic account info TikTok returns (such as the display name), used to confirm the right account is connected.

2. How it's used: only to upload the owner's videos to his own TikTok inbox as drafts.

3. Sharing: nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

4. Storage: the access token is stored privately on the owner's own computer and is never published.

5. Other people's data: the tool does not collect data from viewers or any other TikTok users.

6. Removal: the owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok's settings, and the stored token is then deleted.

7. Contact: open a message to @majorreplay44 on TikTok or YouTube.
